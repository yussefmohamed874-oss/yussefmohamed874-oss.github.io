# برومتات لقطات جيميناي (Veo) — فيديو المياه في الدايت

القاعدة: كل فيديو جيميناي = 10 ثواني = لقطتين (5 ثواني لكل لقطة) في برومت واحد.
بعد الاستلام: أقص كل فيديو عند الثانية 5 وأستخدم كل لقطة منفصلة في موضعها.

## STYLE (يُلصق في نهاية كل برومت)
Vertical 9:16, cinematic photorealistic, shallow depth of field, dark navy-blue background with warm golden rim lighting, high contrast, subtle film grain, smooth slow motion. No text, no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no subtitles. No speech, no music, natural ambient sound only.

## الفيديو 1 — الهوك (بتشرب ميه وانت بتاكل... مقتنع؟)
Shot 1 (0-5s): Close-up of a Middle Eastern man in his 30s at a dinner table mid-meal, lifting a glass of water to his mouth, then pausing with a skeptical, slightly guilty look at the glass. Warm golden practical lights.
Cut to Shot 2 (5-10s): Comedic reaction. The same man freezes in dramatic slow motion, staring at the glass of water as if it were a suspect, eyebrows raised, then slowly slides the glass away from his plate. Fast cinematic push-in.

## الفيديو 2 — الميه مفيهاش سعرات
Shot 1 (0-5s): Extreme macro slow-motion of clear water pouring into a crystal glass, droplets suspended in the air catching warm gold rim light against a deep navy background, tiny bubbles rising.
Cut to Shot 2 (5-10s): A bathroom scale on a tiled floor, bare feet stepping on it, the display blurred and unreadable, then stepping off with a relieved exhale. A glass of water sits beside the scale, glowing in warm light.

## الفيديو 3 — الصوص والعصاير والمشروبات الغازية
Shot 1 (0-5s): Dark moody table. A soda can being opened with fizz and foam spilling over, a sauce bottle squeezing thick sauce over a plate of fries, a juice carton beside them. All packaging plain and generic with no printing. Low-key lighting with golden highlights.
Cut to Shot 2 (5-10s): Top-down shot of a family dinner table full of food. A hand pours sugary soda and drizzles sauce onto every dish in quick slow-motion, vivid warm tones.

## الفيديو 4 — كوبايتين قبل كل وجبة والشبع
Shot 1 (0-5s): Two tall glasses of water side by side on a clean table in front of an empty plate before a meal. A hand lifts the first glass and drinks, then the second. Golden light passing through the glasses.
Cut to Shot 2 (5-10s): Medium shot of a man at the table, smiling and satisfied, gently patting his stomach and pushing the plate slightly away, relaxed and content.

## الفيديو 5 — الهضم وسكر الدم + تخس
Shot 1 (0-5s): Clean cinematic 3D medical visualization. A translucent simplified human torso with the digestive system glowing soft gold, water flowing smoothly through it, dark navy void background, subtle floating particles.
Cut to Shot 2 (5-10s): A person wrapping a measuring tape around their waist, smiling with a proud, confident look. A glass of water sits on the table beside them, soft golden light.

## الفيديو 6 — الخاتمة (الفطار ولا العشا؟)
Shot 1 (0-5s): Bright morning breakfast table with eggs, bread and olives. A hand sets down a glass of water. Warm sunrise light through a window, cozy atmosphere.
Cut to Shot 2 (5-10s): Warm evening dinner table lit by a lamp. A man with a mischievous questioning expression looks at the camera, raising one eyebrow while holding a glass of water as if asking "which one?". Slight comedic push-in.

## ملاحظات
- لو ظهر كتابة أو شعار أو أرقام قراءة في اللقطة، أعد التوليد.
- لقطة التشريح (الفيديو 5 / لقطة 1) محتاجة موافقة الدكتور على شكلها الطبي قبل الاستخدام.
- لو حد جيميناي بيطلع 8 ثواني بدل 10، قولي وأقسم كل فيديو 4+4.
