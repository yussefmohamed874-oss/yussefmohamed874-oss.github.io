# Facebook/TikTok post style (Youssef's reference, Nov 2026)

Use this structure for every post about one of his reels. The reference post was about a stalled weight; its tone is the standard.

## Structure (in this order, one short paragraph each)
1. **Hook:** a question about the viewer's real problem, with 1–2 emojis.
2. **Clinic line:** «من أكتر الحاجات اللي بتتكرر في العيادة 🩺:» followed by a patient quote in «» that ends with 😩.
3. **Bridge:** «خليني أوضحلك إن…» followed by 👇.
4. **Tease:** the number of signs or causes, with no list, so the viewer goes to the reel. One sentence says people often don't notice them 🚫.
5. **Reassurance:** if you do this, it doesn't mean you failed 🙅‍♀️, it means the plan needs adjusting ✨❤️.
6. **Comment question:** 💬 a question about their own case, ending in 👇👇.
7. **Signature (two lines):** «د. يوسف المصري 👨‍⚕️» then «رسام الرشاقة 🎨».
8. **Hashtags:** 7 on one line, chosen for the reel's topic.

## Rules
- **Delivery:** send only the clean post text in one code block, ready to copy into Facebook. No notes or extras inside it. Any comment goes in a separate line after the block.
- Egyptian Arabic, short sentences, feminine address (diet and gym audience).
- Emojis follow his reference, which has more than the skill's default. This is his explicit choice.
- No TikTok link in the post. Upload the video itself to Facebook.
- No promises, no weight-loss numbers, no medication advice, and none of the banned phrases from the brand skill.
- Hashtags are topical (3–4 about the problem, 2–3 about the body part or condition, plus #رسام_الرشاقة). Don't repeat his name in them, since the signature has it.
